# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive project. Be respectful, assume good intent,
disagree with ideas rather than people, and make space for contributors with different backgrounds
and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing
private information, deliberate disruption, and sustained personal attacks.

## Security discussions

Security review can be direct without becoming personal. Challenge assumptions, identify concrete
failure modes, and keep live credentials and private vulnerability details out of public channels.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub profile at https://github.com/danielrob. Project maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected
contributors.
